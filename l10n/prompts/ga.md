# Gaeilge

Translate into Irish. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Technical and plain, as Irish software documentation is written: precise, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Address the reader as "tú" wherever the English says "you"; elsewhere prefer impersonal forms (the autonomous verb).
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.
- Use the terminology of the national terminology database, téarma.ie, where it has a term.

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes “mar sin”, and a quotation inside it takes ‘mar sin’.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, as the glossary writes them, capital letter included, never lenited, eclipsed or prefixed, whatever precedes them. Where the sentence needs a mutation or a case form, put an Irish noun before the term and mutate or inflect that noun: an t-aonán Component, san aonán Release.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular, even where this language does not mark number.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: conradh sóisialta.
- `best practice`: dea-chleachtas, dea-chleachtais; never "riail" or "riachtanas".
- `escape hatch`: slí amach; never "bearna", which reads as a loophole, nor "cúldoras", which reads as a back door.
- `convention`: gnás, as in "gnásanna ainmniúcháin"; never "riail".
- `unopinionated`: gan tuairim dá chuid féin; "mathematically unopinionated" is "go matamaiticiúil gan tuairim dá chuid féin".
- `content-neutral`: neodrach i leith ábhair.
- `agnostic to`: agnóiseach i leith, as in "agnóiseach i leith gnásanna".

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
