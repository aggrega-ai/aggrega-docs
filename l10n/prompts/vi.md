# Tiếng Việt

Translate into Vietnamese. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Technical and plain, as Vietnamese software documentation is written: precise, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Address the reader as "bạn" wherever the English says "you"; leave the subject out where Vietnamese reads better without it.
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes “như vậy”, and a quotation inside it takes ‘như vậy’.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, as the glossary writes them, capital letter included, with a Vietnamese word before the term where the sentence needs one: một Component, các Releases.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular, even where this language does not mark number.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: khế ước xã hội.
- `best practice`: thực hành tốt nhất; never "quy tắc" or "yêu cầu bắt buộc".
- `escape hatch`: lối thoát; never "kẽ hở", which reads as a loophole, nor "cửa sau", which reads as a back door.
- `convention`: quy ước; never "quy tắc", which reads as a rule that is enforced.
- `unopinionated`: không có quan điểm riêng; "mathematically unopinionated" is "về mặt toán học không có quan điểm riêng".
- `mathematically blind`: mù về mặt toán học.
- `dumb`: ngốc: it does what it is given and nothing more; never "đơn giản".
- `blind to`: mù trước, as in "mù trước các quy ước"; never "mù quáng", which reads as reckless.

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
