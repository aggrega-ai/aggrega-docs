# Bahasa Indonesia

Translate into Indonesian. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Formal standard Indonesian, technical and plain, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Address the reader as "Anda" wherever the English says "you"; elsewhere prefer passive forms ("dapat dikonfigurasi").
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes “begini”, and a quotation inside it takes ‘begini’.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, as the glossary writes them, capital letter included. A plural keeps its English form, with a word before it where the sentence needs one, and a term is never repeated to mark a plural: beberapa Components, never Component-Component.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular, even where this language does not mark number.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: kontrak sosial.
- `best practice`: praktik terbaik; never "aturan" or "kewajiban".
- `escape hatch`: jalan keluar; never "celah", which reads as a loophole.
- `convention`: konvensi; never "aturan".
- `unopinionated`: tanpa opini sendiri; "mathematically unopinionated" is "secara matematis tanpa opini sendiri".
- `content-neutral`: netral terhadap konten.
- `agnostic to`: agnostik terhadap, as in "agnostik terhadap konvensi".

## Recurring terms

- manifest, the ordinary word the source writes in lowercase (a Kubernetes manifest, an OCI manifest): manifest, as English writes it; never "manifes".

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
