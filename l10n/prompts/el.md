# Ελληνικά

Translate into Greek. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Technical and plain, as Greek software documentation is written: precise, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Address the reader as "εσείς" wherever the English says "you"; elsewhere prefer impersonal forms ("μπορεί να", "πρέπει να").
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.
- Use the Greek question mark (;) and the ano teleia (·) where Greek punctuation calls for them, except inside inline code.

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes «έτσι», and a quotation inside it takes “έτσι”.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, in Latin letters and as the glossary writes them, capital letter included, never inflected, each with the neuter article: το Aggregate, το Component, το Feature, το FeatureRecipe, το Recipe, το Manifest, το Release, το Provider, το Plugin, το Workload, το SchemaLibrary, το MappingLibrary, το ArtifactLibrary, το WorkloadLibrary.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular, even where this language does not mark number.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: κοινωνικό συμβόλαιο.
- `best practice`: βέλτιστη πρακτική, βέλτιστες πρακτικές; never "κανόνας" or "απαίτηση".
- `escape hatch`: διέξοδος; never "παραθυράκι", which reads as a loophole, nor "πίσω πόρτα", which reads as a back door.
- `convention`: σύμβαση, as in "συμβάσεις ονοματοδοσίας"; never "κανόνας".
- `unopinionated`: χωρίς δικές του απόψεις; "mathematically unopinionated" is "μαθηματικά χωρίς δικές του απόψεις".
- `content-neutral`: ουδέτερος ως προς το περιεχόμενο.
- `agnostic to`: αγνωστικός ως προς, as in "αγνωστικός ως προς τις συμβάσεις".

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
