# Nederlands

Translate into Dutch. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Technical and plain, as Dutch software documentation is written: precise, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Address the reader as "je" wherever the English says "you"; elsewhere prefer impersonal and passive forms.
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.
- Join a kept English term to a Dutch word with a hyphen: Release-bestand, Component-type.

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes “zo”, and a quotation inside it takes ‘zo’.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, as the glossary writes them, capital letter included, with these articles: het Aggregate, de Component, de Feature, de FeatureRecipe, de Recipe, het Manifest, de Release, de Provider, de Plugin, de Workload, de SchemaLibrary, de MappingLibrary, de ArtifactLibrary, de WorkloadLibrary.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular, even where this language does not mark number.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: sociaal contract.
- `best practice`: best practice, kept in English, de best practice, de best practices; never "regel" or "verplichting".
- `escape hatch`: uitweg; never "achterdeur", which reads as a back door, nor "maas", which reads as a loophole.
- `convention`: conventie, as in "naamgevingsconventies"; never "regel" or "voorschrift".
- `unopinionated`: zonder eigen mening; "mathematically unopinionated" is "wiskundig zonder eigen mening".
- `content-neutral`: inhoudsneutraal.
- `agnostic to`: agnostisch ten opzichte van, as in "agnostisch ten opzichte van conventies".

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
