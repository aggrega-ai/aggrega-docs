# Português europeu

Translate into European Portuguese, as written in Portugal. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Technical and plain, as Portuguese software documentation is written: precise, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Where the English says "you", prefer impersonal and third-person forms ("é possível configurar", "pode definir", "o utilizador define"); avoid "tu", and use "você" sparingly, since in Portugal it can read as distant.
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.
- Use European spelling under the 1990 Orthographic Agreement as Portugal applies it (ação, direção, receção, exceção, facto, contacto) and European vocabulary (ficheiro, ecrã, utilizador, registo, aplicação, equipa, sistema operativo, gerir, predefinido), never the Brazilian variant (arquivo, tela, usuário, registro, aplicativo, time, sistema operacional, gerenciar, padrão).
- A continuous action takes "a" and the infinitive ("o compilador está a validar"), not the gerund; an unstressed pronoun follows the verb in a plain affirmative clause ("configura-se", "pode configurá-lo").

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes «assim», and a quotation inside it takes “assim”.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, as the glossary writes them, capital letter included, with these genders: o Aggregate, o Component, a Feature, a FeatureRecipe, a Recipe, o Manifest, a Release, o Provider, o Plugin, o Workload, a SchemaLibrary, a MappingLibrary, a ArtifactLibrary, a WorkloadLibrary.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: contrato social, in the sense of an agreement the people who use something uphold; never a company's articles of association.
- `best practice`: boa prática, the established term with the same force, as boas práticas; never "regra" or "obrigação".
- `escape hatch`: via de escape; never "brecha", which reads as a loophole.
- `convention`: convenção; never "regra" or "norma".
- `unopinionated`: sem opiniões próprias; "mathematically unopinionated" is "matematicamente sem opiniões próprias".
- `content-neutral`: neutro quanto ao conteúdo.
- `agnostic to`: agnóstico em relação a, as in "agnóstico em relação às convenções".

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
