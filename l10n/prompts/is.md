# Íslenska

Translate into Icelandic. Each choice below is a default, made without a native speaker's review, and a native speaker may change any of it.

## Register

- Technical and plain, as Icelandic software documentation is written: precise, with no emphasis the source does not have.
- Address the reader as "þú" wherever the English says "you"; elsewhere prefer impersonal forms ("hægt er að", "þarf að").
- Headings take sentence case: a capital on the first word and on proper nouns only.
- Prefer an established Icelandic technical word over a loanword wherever one exists; the kept terms below are the exception.

## Quotation marks

- A quoted phrase takes „svona“, and a quotation inside it takes ‚svona‘.
- Inline code is never put in quotation marks: it keeps its backticks.

## English terms kept as they are

- The glossary's do-not-translate terms, as the glossary writes them, capital letter included, never inflected. Where the sentence needs a case form or the definite article, join the term to an Icelandic noun with a hyphen and inflect that noun: Component-einingin, í Release-einingunni.
- A kept term's singular and plural are two distinct terms: `Component` and `Components`, `Release` and `Releases`. Write the form the source writes, a plural where the source has the plural and a singular where it has the singular, even where this language does not mark number.
- Everything in inline code, and every YAML key, CLI command and flag, entity kind and file path.
- The names of other products and standards: Kubernetes, Helm, Docker Compose, Terraform, CloudFormation, WebAssembly, JSON Schema, YAML, OCI.
- Abbreviations such as AOT, IR, CLI and ABI stay; their expansion is translated where the source spells it out.

## The qualifiers

- `social contract`: samfélagssáttmáli.
- `best practice`: góð starfsvenja, góðar starfsvenjur; never "regla" or "krafa".
- `escape hatch`: útgönguleið; never "smuga", which reads as a loophole, nor "bakdyr", which reads as a back door.
- `convention`: venja, as in "nafnavenjur"; never "regla".
- `unopinionated`: án eigin skoðana; "mathematically unopinionated" is "stærðfræðilega án eigin skoðana".
- `content-neutral`: hlutlaus gagnvart efni.
- `agnostic to`: óháður, with the dative, as in "óháður venjum".

A native speaker who finds a default here wrong corrects this file; every translation made after the correction follows it.
